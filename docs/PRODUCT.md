# Product Foundation

BeatVault is a focused ecosystem for beatmakers, producers, and independent creators. It combines creation context, a curated platform catalog, previews, creator discovery, and future marketplace, streaming, community, collaboration, releases, analytics, and AI experiences.

The Library is a BeatVault content catalog, not the user's personal hard-drive library. Content enters the platform only through an intentional upload or publishing action. Required metadata and tags belong to the content owner or an administrator; missing required metadata blocks publication.

Shorts is a future, independent discovery domain for beat previews, beatmaking clips, studio sessions, sound design, tips, performances, and creator stories. Its purpose is to connect creators and music, not to reproduce a general-purpose social network. A future Short may reference a creator, beat, track, sample, preview, tags, and marketplace destination through stable relationships.

Phase 1 validates the visual producer workspace loop. Phase 2 establishes product direction and domain boundaries. Streaming, Shorts infrastructure, social features, cloud processing, commerce, and AI remain future work.
